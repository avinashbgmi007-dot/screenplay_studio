INT. STUDY — NIGHT

The desk is a wreck of coffee rings and half-read pages. A single LAMP pools warm light over an open notebook.

MARA (36) sits at the desk. She hasn't moved in an hour. Beside her, a sketchbook — the pages are burned through with small, deliberate holes, like someone pressed a cigarette into the paper.

MARA

I keep writing the same scene.

She crosses it out. Starts again. The pen scratches.

MARA (CONT'D)

It's not that I can't finish it.
It's that I don't want to.

INT. KITCHEN — DAY

Morning light, merciless. DEREK stands at the counter, a mug in hand, watching her across the room.

DEREK

Then stop writing it. Write the scene after it.

MARA looks at him. That's… actually an idea. She hates that it is.

(quietly)

MARA

You sound like someone who's been here.

DEREK

Someone who's been here is here because he didn't stop.
