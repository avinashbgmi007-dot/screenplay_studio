1 · PremiseThe studio as a craftsman's atelier: everything you own is visible from where you stand — instruments on the wall, work on the bench, people in the alcove. Nothing summoned, nothing hunting. 2 · Reader & momentThe writer who works with tools laid out — who wants the dials, drafts and partners permanently at arm's reach, not behind menus. 3 · Emotional registerWarm industry. Plaster walls, oak bench (flat tone, never wood texture), tools worn to fit the hand. The calm of a workshop you know by heart. 4 · Spatial metaphorThree zones: instrument wall left (dials, drafts, stash, queue), manuscript bench center (the page + finding cards in a RESERVED grid slot — never an overlay), partner alcove right. Status rail below. 5 · Type systemSource Serif 4 + Courier Prime + IBM Plex Mono + Caveat for Sameer's hand — the shipped library, re-deployed with workshop discipline. 6 · Chromatic systemPlaster/paper/ink neutrals + toolsteel (instruments) + terracotta/brass/patina severity ramp (all AA on paper) + walnut as Sameer's own stain — partner identity NEVER borrows a severity color (P0 fixed). 7 · Light logicDawn-lamp warmth from the crown; flat material tones below. Depth = 1px lines and single shadows, no textures, no glass. 8 · Navigation modelZones, not tabs: the wall IS the menu; states swap inside the bench; the alcove is always present. Closest to the shipped app's shell — masthead→crown, dock→wall, panel→alcove, 1:1. 9 · Feedback representationTag plates inline on the exact lines + the benchcard parked beside the page in its reserved slot; wall dials summarize; hung notes are the writer's marginalia, clickable to locate. 10 · Partner presenceThe alcove: both seats permanently present with latest lines + unfoldable conversation threads; one canonical surface each (the only partner UI in the file — no duplicates anywhere). 11 · Chat compositionSeats with threads: latest line always visible, history unfolds on demand, and the ask strip addresses Sameer / Sushruta / both with the textarea on paper (12.9:1). 12 · Motion philosophyChisel dots on thinking ("thinking it through" preserved), kilnfill on the running pass, benchcard slide-in beside (never over) the page. Reduced-motion honored. 13 · Composer disciplineAlcove textarea ink-on-plaster 12.9:1; focus = toolsteel ring. All typed text instantly visible (hate-list #5 — the original complaint). 14 · Migration costLowest of the five — the geometry IS the shipped shell refined; every zone maps to an existing region. Evolution, not rebuild. 15 · Risk exposureBeige-on-beige premium risk if contrast discipline slips; the persistent wall must stay sparse or it becomes a dashboard; alcove eats 280px of writing width on small screens. 16 · One-line verdictThe safest evolution and the strongest hate-list compliance — the workshop you already own, rebuilt with a craftsman's discipline.
