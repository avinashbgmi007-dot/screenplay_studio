design notes — 16 attributes
1 · PremiseThe room at 2am: the manuscript IS the interface — full-bleed pages floating in warm dark, everything else summoned on key. The immersion pole of the five. 2 · Reader & momentThe writer deep in a night draft — wants the interface to disappear, trusts a keyboard, finds any chrome at the page's edge an intrusion. 3 · Emotional registerSolitude with company nearby. One lamp's worth of light (paper glow top-center), the room silent except the page. 4 · Spatial metaphorA dark study: the page is the only light object; scene markers are a river of dots at the floor; partners rise from below only when called. 5 · Type systemSource Serif 4 + Courier Prime for the manuscript; IBM Plex Mono for the whisper/palette; Caveat for Sameer and stickies. Same library as the shipped app — zero new fonts. 6 · Chromatic systemWarm near-black room (#0d0b08), one ember accent (#e8a24f) for signals, cool moon (#a9b7c4) as Sushruta's voice; pages are warm paper. Severity = wash + dot only — words never change color (text stays #292219 on paper, 13:1). 7 · Light logicThe page is the light source: radial paper-glow above, ember floor-glow below. Depth = real shadows on the sheet, no UI gradients. 8 · Navigation modelCommand palette (K) is the primary nav — jump, ask, run, export all from one line; the dot-river is the only persistent furniture; the whisper strip fades ONLY when the page is focus (hate-list #3 honored by state). 9 · Feedback representationUnderglow INSIDE the paper: each finding is a colored wash + severity dot on its exact line; hover reveals a margin badge. Nothing covers a word; no side panels of findings anywhere (hate-list #1/#7). 10 · Partner presenceSummoned presences: Sameer rises as a bottom sheet over furniture only (never the page); Sushruta speaks on the moon-lit page. One surface each — the idea state shares the same summoned component, no duplicates (hate-list #10). 11 · Chat compositionHandwritten letters on the page itself (Sameer) / formal reading documents (Sushruta) — not bubbles; composer is a single strip with 11.8:1 text visibility (hate-list #5/#8). 12 · Motion philosophyBreathing: pageIn on state, breathe dots while thinking ("thinking it through" text preserved), presence rise is the only large motion; all reduced-motion honored. 13 · Composer disciplineEvery input dark-bg with light text ≥ 4.99:1; focus = ember border + soft ring — no background flips. The typed text is always the brightest thing in the room. 14 · Migration costHigh — this negates the shipped shell (no masthead, no docks, palette-first). A rebuild, not an evolution; worth it only if immersion is the top criterion. 15 · Risk exposureKeyboard-faith navigation may alienate mouse-first users; the palette must be exhaustive or it feels broken; zero persistent affordances means discoverability rests entirely on the landing copy. 16 · One-line verdictThe purest writing experience of the five and the most radical departure — maximum immersion, maximum migration.
