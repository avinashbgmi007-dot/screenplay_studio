3 Designs — one wireframe, three material languages.

All three obey the same spatial law (44px scene index · 700px continuous manuscript · 380px Context Dock · 28px strip · five states · manuscript dominant · evidence peripheral). They differ on five axes — not palette: swap their colors and fonts, and they still tell apart.

THE SWAP-TEST — with palettes and fonts exchanged, materiality · data objects · density · nav language · hierarchy remain distinct. That is the v1-failure acceptance criterion.
