Six ways to run the studio

These are not mockups — every world runs on your real projects through the live studio: real parses, real analysis reports, real Sameer turns (demo model or your llama-server), and a fix queue whose Dismiss button persists through the app's own storage.

What varies is the information architecture: what each world opens on, how you navigate, where the verdict lives. What stays in all six is the workbench you asked for: script docked center, feedback LEFT, Sameer RIGHT — fold either, or fold both at once.

01

Report-first

the verdict is the home screen

You uploaded the script for the feedback — so the report opens first. Findings drive; the script and Sameer are one click away.

Best when: you want the notes, now

05

Inspector-first

script + context-aware inspector

The script is the screen. Click any scene and the inspector swaps to that scene's findings, characters, and evidence — no separate report room.

06

Command-first

⌘K · the empty desk

Almost nothing on screen. Every surface — scenes, findings, Sameer — is one typed command away. The fastest keyboard workflow.

Best when: you type faster than you click
