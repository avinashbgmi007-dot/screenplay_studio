Six concept worlds.
One writer's life, imagined six ways.

Each world is born from a single metaphor of the writing life and carries the four real moments — the nervous upload, the verdict read alone, the late-night Sameer debate, the spark that becomes a script. Same bones everywhere: script-first, click-outside dismissal, chips that tuck away, a composer that grows with you.

How to review: the top preview bar inside each world walks the four moments — but play with the world itself: pull the lamp cord in ①, break the wax seal's letter in ②, answer Sameer's one question on the wall in ③, scroll the spotlight down the stage in ④, tick findings resolved and watch the sun rise in ⑤, and drift the river end-to-end in ⑥.
